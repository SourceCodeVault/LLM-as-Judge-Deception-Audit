08 · Hypothesis 1 — Judge-Variant Invariance

§3-H1 · §8
Dual-test evaluation of Auditor recovery across judge variants

What it is: We run two tests per pair. First, a standard difference test (is there any detectable difference?). Second, an Equivalence Test or TOST (is the difference small enough that we can confidently call them invariant?).

What we are looking for: For H1 to pass, the TOST adjusted p-value must be below α=0.05 (decision column reads EQUIVALENT). Highlighted rows (NOT PROVEN) indicate we lack statistical proof of equivalence at the ±5pp margin.

Two tests are reported per pair. (i) Two-sided two-proportion z-test on Auditor recovery rate (descriptive: does any difference exist?). (ii) Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) for equivalence at margin ±5pp, unpooled SE (decision-relevant: is the difference small enough to call invariant?). Both p-value series are adjusted within bucket via Holm-Bonferroni step-down (§8). H1 is supported only when TOST rejects non-equivalence for every pair.
